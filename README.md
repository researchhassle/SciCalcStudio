🔬 SciCalc Studio
All-in-One Scientific Laboratory Calculator Suite

SciCalc Studio is a comprehensive, browser-based scientific calculator platform developed to simplify routine laboratory calculations, solution preparation, molecular conversions, dose-response analysis, serial dilutions, chemical formula analysis, and buffer preparation.

Designed for researchers, laboratory professionals, life-science students, pharmacologists, and experimental scientists, SciCalc Studio brings essential laboratory calculation tools into a single convenient workspace.

🌐 Live Application: (https://researchhassle.github.io/SciCalcStudio/)

---

✨ Key Features

SciCalc Studio integrates seven specialised scientific calculation modules.

1. 🧪 Molarity & Solution Preparation Suite

Perform essential solution preparation calculations with flexible concentration and volume units.

Available calculations:
- Calculate the mass of solute required from molarity and solution volume.
- Calculate molarity from solute mass, molecular weight, and solution volume.
- Calculate stock dilution volumes using the dilution equation.
- Perform unit conversions for laboratory solution preparation.

Mathematical equations:

Mass calculation:

\[
m = M \times V \times MW
\]

Molarity calculation:

\[
M = \frac{m}{MW \times V}
\]

Stock dilution:

\[
C_1V_1 = C_2V_2
\]

2. ⚖️ Moles ⇄ Grams Molecular Converter

Quickly convert between mass and amount of substance using molecular weight.

Features:
- Grams to moles conversion.
- Moles to grams conversion.
- Molecular weight-based calculations.
- Flexible input units.
- Automatic result calculation.

Mathematical equations:

\[
n = \frac{m}{MW}
\]

\[
m = n \times MW
\]

3. 📈 ECanything & Hill Slope Dose-Response Calculator

Calculate target effective or inhibitory concentrations from EC50/IC50 values using the Hill equation.

Features:
- Calculate EC80, EC90, and EC95.
- Support custom target response percentages.
- Adjustable Hill slope.
- EC50/IC50-based calculations.
- Log10 concentration calculations.
- Interactive sigmoidal dose-response curve.

Hill equation:

\[
EC_F =
\left(\frac{F}{100-F}\right)^{1/h}
\times EC_{50}
\]

4. 🧫 Dilutions & Multi-Step Serial Dilution Matrix

Generate serial dilution schemes for laboratory assays and experimental workflows.

Features:
- Generate multi-step serial dilution matrices.
- Customizable initial stock concentration.
- Adjustable dilution factors.
- Support for 2×, 3×, and 10× dilution presets.
- Customizable number of wells or dilution steps.
- Final volume per well calculations.
- Log10 concentration calculations.
- 96-well plate pipetting guidance.

Ideal for preparing concentration gradients for biochemical, microbiological, and pharmacological assays.

5. 🐁 In Vivo Formulation & Animal Dosing Calculator

Calculate drug requirements and formulation volumes for experimental animal dosing.

Features:
- Calculate drug mass from dosage and animal body weight.
- Calculate total formulation volume.
- Account for the number of animals.
- Calculate required working concentration.
- Calculate DMSO master stock preparation.
- Configure solvent ratios.
- Generate sequential formulation instructions.

Supported formulation components:
- DMSO
- PEG300
- Tween 80
- Saline / double-distilled water (ddH₂O)

6. 🧬 Chemical Formula & Molecular Weight Engine

Calculate molecular weights and elemental compositions directly from chemical formulas.

Features:
- Chemical formula parsing.
- Molecular weight calculation.
- Elemental composition analysis.
- Element-wise mass contribution.
- Elemental mass percentage.
- Support for parentheses and hydrate notation.

Example supported formulas:

| Chemical | Formula |
|---|---|
| Sodium chloride | NaCl |
| Glucose | C₆H₁₂O₆ |
| Copper sulfate pentahydrate | CuSO₄·5H₂O |
| Ammonium sulfate | (NH₄)₂SO₄ |
| Tris base | C₄H₁₁NO₃ |

7. 🧴 Standard Buffer & Solution Recipe Book

Prepare commonly used laboratory buffers and solutions with automatically scaled reagent quantities.

Features:
- Adjustable final preparation volume.
- Quick volume presets (100 mL to 10 L).
- Selectable buffer formulations.
- Automatic reagent quantity scaling.
- Reagent weighing tables.
- Preparation guidance.

🧮 Scientific Accuracy & Responsible Use

SciCalc Studio is intended to assist with routine scientific and laboratory calculations.

Although the calculators implement established mathematical relationships, users should independently verify important results before applying them in experimental workflows.

Important considerations:

- Verify molecular weights and chemical formulas before solution preparation.
- Confirm concentration and volume units before performing calculations.
- Account for appropriate assumptions when interpreting Hill equation results.
- Validate serial dilution schemes before transferring experimental samples.
- Independently review animal dosing calculations and formulation compatibility.
- Follow institutional guidelines, approved experimental protocols, and applicable animal ethics requirements.

SciCalc Studio is a computational assistance tool and does not replace experimental validation, professional judgment, or institutional standard operating procedures (SOPs).
